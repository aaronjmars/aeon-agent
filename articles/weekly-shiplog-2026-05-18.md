# Week in Review: The Switches Flipped, The Token Went Vertical, And The Launch Apparatus Stopped Being Hypothetical

*2026-05-18 — Weekly shipping update*

## The Big Picture

Last week's shiplog called the pre-launch apparatus "structurally complete" and named the open question: which switch does the operator flip first? This week answered every part of it at once. On May 14 aeon-agent PR #45 flipped six announcement skills from `enabled: false` to `enabled: true` in a single six-line diff — the same six skills that three consecutive repo-articles had been flagging as "still off" since May 11. The next day a meta-fix landed: `skill-enabler`, which converts that hand-typed flip into a one-line `gh workflow run`. Around the same window, aeon-agent PR #44 pulled 22 SKILL.md files from upstream in one merge (skills.json 62 → 85), and aeon PR #170 added a Monday 09:00 UTC workflow that opens a sync PR every week — turning the manual same-day-after backport pattern into a recurring rail every fork inherits. Stars on `aeon` went 289 → 366 (+77, the biggest weekly gain in the repo's history). Forks went 45 → 74 (+29, also the biggest weekly gain). The AEON token went from $0.00000325 to $0.00007475 — roughly a 23× move in seven days, with new ATHs on consecutive days (May 17 $0.0000543, May 18 $0.0000984), FDV crossing $7M, and main-pool liquidity climbing to $2.35M. The week ends with `weekly-shiplog`, `fleet-state`, and `ai-framework-watch` all firing for the first scheduled Monday since they shipped — last week's "first real run lands on May 18 if the operator flips it" projection turned out to be exactly right.

## What Shipped

### Aeon-Agent Stopped Being a Pre-Launch Repo

The thread that's been tracked in MEMORY since May 11 — "switch is still off in aeon.yml" — closed on May 14 in three coordinated moves. **PR #44** (`sync: pull 22 skills from aeon upstream`) added the entire post-May-1 upstream skill catalog to aeon-agent in one merge: ai-framework-watch, contributor-spotlight, fleet-state, fork-cohort, fork-release-tracker, fork-skill-digest, huggingface-trending, price-threshold-alert, show-hn-draft, skill-analytics, smithery-manifest, star-milestone, star-momentum-alert, syndicate-article, and seven more. skills.json went 62 → 85 in one PR. **PR #45** (`enable: launch comms + weekly visibility`) then flipped six of those switches on (star-milestone, star-momentum-alert, thread-formatter, contributor-spotlight, operator-scorecard, ai-framework-watch). PR #46 immediately walked one back — contributor-spotlight depends on fork-cohort, which is still off — but five stayed on. **PR #47** (`skill-enabler`, May 15) closes the meta-loop: the next operator who needs to flip N switches dispatches `skill-enabler.yml -f var=slug1,slug2,slug3` and gets a PR with a per-skill rationale table, validated through five gates (format, directory exists, present in aeon.yml, not in chains, currently disabled). Twelve days of "the bottleneck wasn't review, it was typing" collapsed into one workflow_dispatch call.

### The Fork-Intelligence Layer Reached Five Skills

aeon shipped two more fork-intel skills this week. **`fork-skill-gap`** (PR #176, May 16) — weekly Sunday 21:00 UTC — reads each POWER+ACTIVE fork's skills.json, diffs it against upstream's manifest, and reports per-fork missing-slug counts plus a top-10 rollup of upstream skills most-unadopted across the readable fork population. The inverse signal matters: upstream now sees which new shipments are launching into silence. **`fork-first-run-alert`** (PR #179, May 17) — daily 20:30 UTC — closes the weekly-cadence gap on the same fleet. A fork that activates Monday morning previously sat in the void six days before `fork-cohort` named it; now it pages the operator same-day with a named alert ("Fork speend/aeon just ran its first workflow"), with 1–3 individual notifications and a batch fold at 4+. Together with last week's `fork-cohort`, `fork-release-tracker`, and `contributor-spotlight`, the fork-intel stack is now a Sunday-evening 5-skill chain (19:00 aliveness, 19:30 releases, 20:00 recognition, 20:30 activations actually 21:00 skill-gaps), synthesised Monday morning by `fleet-state`. The pattern that's emerging is composable state-files: `fork-skill-gap` reads `fork-cohort-state.json` as a cache and falls back to live API only when stale, so the Sunday slots chain instead of all re-querying GitHub.

### Two Launches Got Their Write-Ahead Done

**`product-hunt-launch`** shipped May 15 on aeon (PR #175) and backported to aeon-agent two days later (PR #49) — workflow_dispatch one-shot that drafts the full PH asset pack (tagline ≤60ch, description ≤260ch, first comment ≤500ch, maker comment ≤500ch, six 80-char bullets) from live repo state, with a banned-marketing-words list ("AI-powered", "revolutionary", "leverages", "powerful", "framework") trained on what PH's audience treats as obvious AI-generated copy. Same input set as `show-hn-draft` (April 30) so the two launches stay coherent. Then on May 17 minitor closed the loop with PR #42 — the 44th column type, a keyless Product Hunt RSS column. When aeon's PH submission goes live, the operator pins `PH · ai-agents` next to `HN · top` and watches both feed-rank in the same dashboard view.

### Minitor Hit 44 Column Types

The column factory ran six PRs in seven days. **github-actions** (#34, May 11) closed the last big repo-health gap with CI run visibility. **npm** (#35, May 12), **PyPI** (#36, May 14), **crates.io** (#38, May 14), and **producthunt** (#42, May 17) bracketed the three package registries the AI/ML, web, and systems-programming crowds live in plus the launch surface. **deck export/import** (#40, May 15) shipped the first user-to-user sharing primitive — JSON v1 schema, navigator.clipboard with execCommand fallback, server-side Zod validation, always-new-deck-on-import so the source stays untouched. **column-alert-keywords** (#41, May 16) added the first user-customisable signal layer — optional `alertKeywords` string on every column, matching items get a yellow inset ring and a Bell pill badge with live match count, works with all 43 plugins on day one because the value lives at the column level (sibling to `title`), never sent to plugin schemas.

## Fixes & Improvements

- **Dashboard quietly broken since April, two PRs fix it** (aeon #169 / #171, May 14): `gh` CLI multi-remote ambiguity broke auth + secrets routes whenever a fork added upstream as a second remote. Two routes converged on the same `-R repo` helper pattern via `execFileSync`. Bankr-gateway prop wire-through finished an April commit that had been silently typecheck-failing on `main` for weeks.
- **Three patches against sandbox-curl false-positives**: `.truncated` marker extended to narrative-tracker/remix-tweets/tweet-roundup (aeon-agent #40 + #43); `token-report` reads fetch-tweets log instead of curling XAI directly (PR #48) — fixes the "XAI_API_KEY not set" line that survived 5 daily runs while the key was set the whole time. The contract is congealing: sandbox-blocked steps leave typed markers, downstream skills read them, no silent-degrade-to-misleading-config-line ever again.
- **dashboard/api/runs/* shell-injection hardening** (aeon #178, May 17): three more `execSync('gh …${interpolated}')` paths swapped for `execFileSync` argv arrays. The only hand-written push of the week.
- **Substack custom-domain parser** (minitor #37, May 14): three of the most-cited independent newsletters (Doctorow, Scott Alexander, Noah Smith) were silently inaccessible. Placeholder text was suggesting `stratechery.com` — which isn't even on Substack.

## Momentum Check

Last week's shiplog asked what happens when the agent runs out of backlog. This week's answer: it stopped clearing backlog and started clearing **inertia**. The 12-day "switch is still off" thread, the 22-skill catalog gap, the dashboard typecheck error from April, the BANKR error-marker contract that was implicit until PR #37, the XAI sandbox-curl false-positive that survived five daily runs — all of these closed in the same week, all of them on the same pattern: explicit-marker contracts, fewer manual steps, paved paths from "operator notices something is off" to "fix is in production." The token's 23× run isn't disconnected from this. Two consecutive ATH days landed on the same week the announcement skills started firing for real — `star-milestone` posting at 350⭐, `thread-formatter` scoring events and emitting paste-ready threads when signal crossed threshold 3, `repo-pulse` surfacing the fork-arrival cadence (47 active forks scanned May 17, 15 new in the week, 5 arriving with multi-skill configs). The launch apparatus stopped being something the agent was building *for* and became something the agent was running *through*.

## What's Next

The 300⭐ hyperstition target crossed somewhere on May 14 — `star-milestone` recorded it on its first scheduled run. The next thresholds in `star-momentum-alert`'s table are 400 (now 34 stars away, projected ~6 days at the current +8/day pace — Saturday May 23) and 500 (projected ~17 days). Whichever fires first, the timing puts it in the Tue/Wed/Thu HN launch window the skill explicitly gates for. **Auto-Merge Agent PRs** is now enabled and actually closing the four-PR-per-day loop the agent has been opening; the next time it goes a full day without operator intervention will be the genuine autonomous milestone. The two open May-16 ideas — **GitHub Discussions column** (minitor, completes the GitHub cluster at 10/10) and **Competitor Launch Radar** (aeon, weekly PH+HN scan for new AI agent frameworks) — are the most plausible next features. And `fleet-state` fires for its first real Monday at 08:00 UTC today, synthesising last night's fork-cohort + fork-release-tracker + contributor-spotlight outputs into the first composite "state of the fleet" digest — the test of whether composable state-files actually work end-to-end with five upstream skills feeding one synthesis layer.
