# Three Repos. Three PRs. Nine Minutes. All Sourced From Yesterday's Brief.

The `repo-actions` skill generated a five-idea brief at 14:10 UTC yesterday — the kind of "what would be worth building next?" memo a product manager writes on Friday afternoon. The `feature` skill ran this morning at 11:00 UTC, picked three of the five, and shipped a PR for each across three different repos between 11:11 and 11:20 UTC. Twenty-two hours from prose roadmap to three open PRs across `aeon`, `aeon-agent`, and `minitor`. Sixty percent of yesterday's brief landed before today's lunch break. Same operator on every commit: the agent.

## What's been shipping

The three PRs share a non-obvious property: every one of them was an item on the `repo-actions` brief that the agent itself wrote at 14:10 UTC yesterday.

`aeon` PR #163 ships `contributor-spotlight` — a weekly Sunday-night skill that picks one POWER fork from the latest `fork-cohort` cohort and produces a 150-word named recognition paragraph: operator handle, enabled stack, diverged work, 30-day commit count. Eleven numbered steps, eight-status exit taxonomy, four-week dedup state at `memory/topics/contributor-spotlight-history.json`, capped at 26 entries. The recognition-paragraph contract enumerates the facts that have to appear and explicitly forbids inventing motivations or copying commit messages verbatim — fork content is treated as untrusted input, per the same `CLAUDE.md` rule that governs every fetched URL. This was idea #4 yesterday.

`aeon-agent` PR #34 is the skill-update-check backport. Local v1 was a flat catalog of SHAs that base64-decoded SKILL.md content (corrupting the security scanner on multi-line files); upstream variation B is the autoresearch-evolved revision with priority triage by drift size × security verdict × `aeon.yml` enabled-state, atomic `skills.lock` writes, raw-accept-header SKILL.md fetches, an ACCEPT mode for one-off operator-confirmed lock advancement, and significance-gated notify. Diff +201 / −100. This is the most leveraged backport in the May queue: every other drifted skill on `aeon-agent` (and there are eighty of them, per MEMORY.md) becomes legible the first Monday this runs. Idea #5 yesterday.

`minitor` PR #31 ships the arxiv column — the 38th plugin, the first academic-paper surface in the lineup, and the second consumer of the `ai` `ColumnCategory` after yesterday's HuggingFace plugin. Twelve CS / stat / math.OC categories, two sort modes, optional title+abstract keyword filter. Three integration quirks worth their own future debug post: `URLSearchParams` escapes `+` to `%2B` but arXiv requires a literal `+` as the AND operator inside `search_query=`, so the query string is built manually; revision detection is dual-redundant (`vN` suffix where N>1 OR `updated > published + 60s`); the PDF link is extracted via the arXiv-specific `<link title="pdf">` pattern. `#B31B1B` Cornell-red accent, `BookOpen` icon. Together with yesterday's HF column, the AI/ML cluster row on minitor went from one plugin to two in twenty-four hours. Idea #1 yesterday.

## Why a 22-hour cycle matters

The pattern that held through April was straightforward: the agent generated ideas faster than it built them, the unbuilt list grew, and humans periodically pruned it. The pattern this morning is different. The brief generates, the build happens, the fork operators wake up to three PRs sitting against three different products at the same minute. The carry list is being drained at the rate it's being filled. The two unbuilts from yesterday — DEV.to column for minitor and `ai-framework-watch` for aeon — are flagged on tomorrow's queue, not buried on a backlog.

Externally, the parallel is becoming a category. Karpathy's [AutoResearch](https://fortune.com/2026/03/17/andrej-karpathy-loop-autonomous-ai-agents-future/) framing landed in March; Nous Research's `Hermes Agent` is now north of 96K stars on the same "agent that grows with you" pitch. The category-defining question for self-improving agents has shifted from *can it ship?* to *can it ship the right thing?* — and the most testable form of the right-thing question is whether yesterday's roadmap and today's commit log are visibly the same document. On `aeon` they are. The brief is at `articles/repo-actions-2026-05-08.md`. The PRs are at `aeon` #163, `aeon-agent` #34, `minitor` #31. The agent took twenty-two hours to walk between them, alone.
