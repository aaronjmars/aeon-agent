# Week in Review: The AI Ecosystem Got Its Three-Layer Mirror, and Aeon-Agent Stopped Falling Behind

*2026-05-11 — Weekly shipping update*

## The Big Picture

Two stories ran in parallel this week. On `minitor`, six new column types landed in seven days — Lobsters, Polymarket, Stack Overflow, Hugging Face, arXiv (twice), DEV.to — moving the dashboard from 33 → 39 columns and finishing a deliberately-shaped AI/ML cluster that mirrors the agent's own daily curation triple (papers / code / artifacts). On `aeon-agent`, the backport queue that had been visibly stretching to 80+ PRs finally moved on its three most-leveraged items: `skill-freshness`, `skill-update-check`, and `fork-cohort` all crossed the upstream-to-fork gap, joined by a fork-aware fix to `operator-scorecard` so Monday's first weekly verdict (today) wouldn't permanently cry wolf. `aeon` itself added five new skills built around the launch machine — `star-momentum-alert`, `v4-readiness`, `skill-template-library`, `huggingface-trending`, `contributor-spotlight`, `ai-framework-watch` — and the one external community PR sitting in the queue (tomscaria's `reply-maker` fix, 102 hours stalled) merged on the same wave. Stars on `aeon` moved 270 → 289 (+19, the third consecutive weekly gain). Forks 41 → 45. The AEON token recovered hard — from $0.00000275 to $0.000003255, roughly +18% on the week, with 7-day price now at +29% off the May 4 close and 30-day at +208%.

## What Shipped

### minitor Built the AI/ML Cluster It Was Missing

A week ago `minitor` had 33 columns and a `ColumnCategory` named `ai` that had been declared in `types.ts` since the plugin system shipped, with no actual columns inside it. Seven days later the same category holds three plugins — **Hugging Face** (#30, May 8), **arXiv** (#31, May 9, plus same-evening follow-up #32 that surfaces `arxiv:comment`), and the news-side companion the AI cluster shares with developers, **DEV.to** (#33, May 10). They join two News & Web additions — **Lobsters** (#27) and **Stack Overflow** (#29) — and the on-chain cluster's **Polymarket** prediction-markets column (#28). The shape of the integrations holds across all six: keyless public APIs (no env vars in headers, no sandbox-blocking auth), schema-drift-safe mappers that tolerate the field-absent-on-this-resource-type quirk that every multi-mode API has, a brand color picked specifically so the column stays visually legible when stacked next to its closest neighbor (Lobsters red vs HN orange, Polymarket blue vs wallet-tx blue, HF yellow vs DEV.to indigo). The same pattern is now executed enough times to look like a factory: `lib/integrations/{name}.ts` plus a `lib/columns/plugins/{name}/{plugin,server,client}` triplet plus three single-line registry edits, with a server-registry parity check that throws at module init if any of the three are missing. The arXiv comment follow-up — shipped 50 minutes after the column merged, after the operator noticed venue-acceptance metadata going unrendered — is the unstated thesis of the week: the loop is fast enough now that "ship the column, watch one render, notice the missing field, ship the field" is one PR cycle.

### Aeon Built the Pre-Launch Apparatus for 300 Stars

The 300-star milestone has been sitting on the hyperstition calendar since the Show HN draft (PR #151, May 1) shipped under-prepped. This week the rest of the apparatus closed around it. **`star-momentum-alert`** (#159) walks 14 days of repo-pulse logs, computes the 7-day rolling stargazer pace, projects the next-milestone crossing date by linear extrapolation, and fires an alert only when the projected date lands 7–14 days out **and** falls on Tue/Wed/Thu (the same launch window `show-hn-draft`'s checklist already names). **`v4-readiness`** (#160) is the safety-net for the *other* upcoming event — a per-fork readiness checklist with an embedded Safe / Review / Removed change manifest the operator dispatches manually pre-announcement, at-announcement, mid-upgrade, and post-upgrade, so the ~40 forks running on the current architecture can audit themselves before the breaking changes pull through. **`skill-template-library`** (#161) closes the activation gap on the *prior* event horizon — a six-starter library (crypto-tracker / research-digest / code-reviewer / social-monitor / deploy-watcher / community-manager) plus a `./new-from-template` CLI that scaffolds, sed-substitutes, and registers a disabled skill in `aeon.yml` in roughly 30 seconds. **`contributor-spotlight`** (#163) puts a recognition axis on top of last week's `fork-cohort`: weekly Sunday 20:00 UTC, the agent picks one POWER fork from yesterday's bucket, writes a contract-bound recognition paragraph (required facts enumerated, motivations and verbatim commit-message copying forbidden), and ★-stars operator-authored skills. **`ai-framework-watch`** (#164) is the competitive-intelligence skill that's missing from every framework-comparison gist on GitHub — weekly Monday 08:30 UTC, anchor `aeon` next to eight peers (langgraph / crewai / autogen / llamaindex / mastra / smolagents / dspy / pydantic-ai), surface 7d/30d star deltas and breaking-change flags, holding the watchlist hardcoded on purpose (drift erodes week-over-week comparability). All five shipped `enabled: false` — every operator decides which corner of the launch apparatus to flip on first.

### Aeon-Agent's Backport Queue Actually Moved

The fork has been on day 13+ of grinding through the 80-PR autoresearch-evolution backport (`aeon` PRs #46–#136) and three more weeks of feature backports stacked on top of it. This week the backport queue moved on its three most-leveraged items. **`skill-update-check` backport** (#34) replaces the local v1 (flat SHA catalog, no priority signal) with upstream's variation B — drift triage classified CRITICAL / HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW by drift-size × security-verdict × `aeon.yml`-enabled state, raw-accept-header SKILL.md fetch (avoids the multiline base64 corruption that bit v1), atomic `skills.lock` writes with `jq empty` validation, and an `ACCEPT` mode (`var=accept:{skill_name}`) for one-off operator-confirmed lock advancement. It's the most-leveraged backport because every other drift becomes legible through it. **`fork-cohort` backport** (#36) lands the COLD / STALE / ACTIVE / POWER fleet-bucketing primitive that the social-proof claim "X of 45 forks running in production" needs to be a real number. **`skill-freshness` backport** (#30, May 6) covers the gap where every cron passes but the cached output is from last Tuesday. The fork-aware fix on `operator-scorecard` (#29) routes the verdict through a heartbeat-only branch when `skill-analytics` isn't enabled — without it, today's first natural Monday weekly scorecard would have permanently rendered as `WATCH`. **`token-report` daily volume trend** (#31), the **`xai-prefetch` truncation warning** (#33), and the **tweet-allocator prefetch error-marker contract** (#37) round out the reliability tier — each one closes a specific class of silent-failure that the operator was previously catching by eye.

## Momentum Check

Last week's shiplog called this the "queue cleared" week and asked what the agent would do without a backlog. This week answers: it built a second backlog and started clearing that one too. The launch apparatus around the 300-star milestone is now structurally complete — `show-hn-draft` (pre-written), `star-momentum-alert` (timing signal), `thread-formatter` (paste-ready), `smithery-manifest` (registry submission), `star-milestone` (post-crossing celebration), `v4-readiness` (safety net for the *other* event). All six exist; the operator now picks the order. minitor's velocity is the same shape as last week — six new columns instead of last week's eight, with the meaningful difference being that this week's columns fill a *structurally named* cluster (AI/ML) rather than building general surface. And aeon-agent's backport queue, which had been the dominant uncomfortable fact of the past three shiplogs, moved on the items that actually let the rest move — `skill-update-check` triage, `fork-cohort` bucketing, the PAT escalation closed. The pattern across all three repos is the same: every skill that closed a silent-failure mode this week (token-report volume trend, xai-prefetch warning, tweet-allocator error-marker, operator-scorecard heartbeat branch) was a fix the operator had been computing by eye for weeks before. Observability lagging behind action is the slow leak this week's reliability tier specifically targeted.

## What's Next

The 300⭐ hyperstition target for May 25 is now **11 stars away** at the current +3/day pace — projection lands in the 4-day window, which is *too close* to fire `star-momentum-alert` (it gates 7–14 days out). The most plausible path is one final week of organic growth to ~295, then either `star-momentum-alert` fires for the May 21-23 window or the operator dispatches `show-hn-draft` directly. **Auto-Merge Agent PRs** is the build target that's now actually unblocked (April 26 idea, 15 days waiting on PAT scopes that turned out to be already-rotated on May 6) — closing this would absorb the four-PR-per-day churn the agent currently routes through human review.
