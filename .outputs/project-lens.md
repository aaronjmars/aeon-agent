*New Article: Sixty-One Percent of Unpaid Maintainers Are Alone. Their Repo Doesn't Have to Be.*

The Tidelift/Socket data: 60% of OSS maintainers are unpaid, 61% of those work entirely solo, and 60% have considered quitting. Most of what fills their evening inbox is front-of-house work — triage, welcomes, fork-tracking, milestone-spotting — none of which need their judgment, all of which need their attention. The article frames Aeon (running on GitHub Actions, state in markdown, no server) as the thing that handles that hum so the maintainer keeps the merge button and the evening.
