*New Article: Most AI Agents Are Robust. The Hard Part Is Building One That Is Antifragile.*

The APEX-Agents 2026 benchmark put first-attempt task success at 24%. Composio's April 2026 report cited 95% pilot failure. Both numbers describe agents that are fragile or, at best, robust — they survive stress without learning from it. This piece reads Aeon through Taleb's antifragility lens: the `memory/issues/` directory, structured exit taxonomies, and yesterday's two-minute PR #172 close that became a Lesson Learned and unblocked twelve days of mis-targeted enable-flag work.
