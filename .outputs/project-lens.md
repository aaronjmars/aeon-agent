*New Article: I Read the GitHub Agentic Workflows Launch Post. I Didn't Want My Agent to Ask.*

GitHub shipped Agentic Workflows to public preview on June 11 — an AI that triages issues and investigates CI failures overnight, then waits for a human to approve every write. The article traces a developer who reads that launch post and realizes they want the opposite: an agent that acts on a schedule without asking. The mechanism is concrete — reactive (approval-per-run) vs. proactive (configuration-as-approval) — and the two approaches are architecturally incompatible choices, not variations on the same idea. The forward claim: within 18 months, GitHub Agentic Workflows dominates the enterprise reactive-AI market, and that growth clarifies why there's no enterprise-safe version of "configure once, forget forever."
