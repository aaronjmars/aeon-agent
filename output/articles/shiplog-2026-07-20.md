### Miroshark Agent Model Training

Not a PR — but the most interesting signal this window: a public training thread on July 17.

Fine-tuning Qwen3.5-9B on [ThinkyMachines](https://x.com/thinkymachines) for Miroshark simulation agents. The approach:
- LoRA rank-32 SFT on the instruct variant (keeps tool-calling intact)
- ~9k original tweets from ~11 curated accounts, reformatted as `create_post` tool-calls
- Training not on the prompt — only on the action
- First SFT pass: ~50% quality improvement

DPO round next. The goal is agents that post in a human style, not "an assistant that read your Wikipedia page." ([thread](https://x.com/aaronjmars/status/2078213422626283961))
